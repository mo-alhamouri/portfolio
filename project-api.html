Case Study — Commercial Real Estate API

An API Platform for Commercial Real Estate

Industry: Financial Services, Real Estate, Title Insurance

A leading provider of title insurance, settlement services, and risk solutions for real estate and mortgage transactions. The company plays a critical role in ensuring property ownership integrity and enabling secure real estate transactions across the U.S.

Building a programmatic bridge to streamline the title process for 3rd party applications.

Role Lead Product Designer
Platform API Platform / Developer Portal
Tools Figma, Miro, API Documentation

Problem Statement

How might we programmatically connect with commercial sector services, allowing 3rd parties to streamline the title process by building them into their applications?

Role & Responsibilities

As the Lead Product Designer, I spearheaded the product’s design strategy, overseeing the full design lifecycle — from initial discovery workshops to final implementation. I led a small design team and partnered closely with product managers and engineers to create intuitive user journeys for the mortgage and real estate sectors.

I also drove multiple post-MVP iterations, improving usability, performance, and adoption based on customer feedback and business goals.

Jam Sessions & Defining the Users

Based on the user information that we learned in the workshops, I imagined the ideal personas that would fit the scenario perfectly and assigned a list of expectations to them. The first persona is for a technology lead working at a marketplace company for commercial real estate—I called the first persona Jessie. They are looking for an API platform with clear documentation guide that would help their company manage deals and orders with access to live data and ease of integration into their application.

Workshops & User Journey

Along with the personas, I created a journey map divided into three phases: Discovery Phase, Application Development Phase, and Application Release Phase. Each phase contained the steps that the persona would take to achieve their goal.

Wireframes

Here we can see low-fi designs for the developer portal, specifically the onboarding experience. It should be quick and easy and should require basic information. After submitting the request, the user needs to confirm their email address and then they will get a key and a password to access the API documentation portal and start testing the APIs.

I validated the feasibility of the concepts with the front end engineers before moving to high-fi designs, and with the PM to ensure the user stories and business requirements were met.

High-Fidelity Designs

In the high-fidelity design phase, it's time to focus on the design details. I ensure that the paddings and margins are pixel perfect. I review the designs done by the other product designers on the team to ensure consistency in patterns and overall user experience. At this stage, I ensure that all components, colours and text styles are linked to the design system.

Before going live with the designs, I prototyped the main flows and interactions. I also love to be part of the user testing sessions to observe how users are reacting to the design and how easy it is for them to navigate through it.

Admin Dashboard

Designed for internal teams to manage deal flows, monitor API usage, and oversee organizational health with granular control.

Developer Portal

A streamlined experience for developers to access documentation, manage keys, and test endpoints in a sandbox environment.

Marketing Website

The public-facing side of the platform, focused on clear value propositions, trust, and converting enterprise leads.
