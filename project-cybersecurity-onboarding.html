Case Study — Cybersecurity Onboarding

First-Time User Onboarding Experience

Industry: Cybersecurity, Software

A global cybersecurity company that develops digital protection solutions for consumers and enterprises. Its products safeguard devices and data against malware, phishing, and online threats, helping users stay secure across personal and professional digital environments.

Role Lead Product Designer
Platform Cross-Platform (Desktop / Mobile)
Tools Figma, Prototype, User Testing

Problem Statement

How could we make an easy and visually appealing journey for the user to get started with internet security protection?

Role & Responsibilities

As the Lead Product Designer, I directed user experience and visual design across the full product lifecycle — from research and ideation to production. I conducted competitor analysis, led A/B testing, and guided a small team of designers through prototyping and design documentation.

I partnered closely with product managers and engineering leads to translate business requirements into high-quality, security-focused user experiences.

High-Fidelity Designs

Once the low-fidelity concepts were validated, I moved to high-fidelity designs consistent with McAfee’s visual identity and design system. I focused on clear layouts, intuitive navigation, and visual hierarchy to support first-time users.

Working closely with engineering, I ensured the designs were technically feasible and user-ready, using prototypes to demonstrate key interactions and guide implementation.
