01 — Challenge

Problem Statement

Problem statement
How might we improve retention by adding new features that could attract new users to register and use the mobile app as well as attract existing users to stay active?
02 — Role

Role & Responsibilities

As the Lead Product Designer, I led a multidisciplinary design squad of three designers, a PM, an illustrator, and five engineers to design and deliver a rewards system that improved user retention.

I defined the design strategy, guided concept development, and oversaw usability validation. I also worked with data teams to track engagement post-launch, using insights to optimize rewards mechanics over time.

03 — Insights

Research, Options & Trade-offs

What research revealed

Users engaged most with rewards they could use immediately; long point-accumulation goals lost them.

Directions explored

  • Option A: A points-only loyalty scheme. Dropped: too slow to feel rewarding.
  • Option B: Short daily activities and instant rewards on top of points. Chosen.

Trade-offs

Partner offers depended on commercial agreements, so the design used a flexible offer template rather than bespoke layouts.

04 — Design

High-Fidelity Designs

A beautiful and joyful user interface packed with many fun activities. A rewarding experience made for everyone. Play, enjoy, and get rewarded.
